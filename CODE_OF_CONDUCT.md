# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Dim Veil community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and contributor time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publishing private information, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when appropriate, or contact the maintainer through the repository. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation to protect the community.

This is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
